  <section class="homepage-research" aria-labelledby="research-title">
    <h2 id="research-title">Research interests</h2>
    <p>
      My research focuses on discrete problems in modern computational models,
      particularly in distributed computing. I am interested in understanding
      what networks can compute efficiently when information and communication
      are local.
    </p>
    <p>
      Beyond my main research area, I am also interested in connections between
      local algorithms and other domains, such as optimization and cryptography,
      and in discrete and continuous algorithms for modelling real-world problems.
    </p>
  </section>
